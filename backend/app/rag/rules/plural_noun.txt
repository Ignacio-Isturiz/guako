id: plural_noun.regular_plural
error_type: plural_noun
level: A1
title: Regular plurals
rule: Most nouns add -s (cat-cats). Nouns ending in -s, -x, -z, -ch, -sh add -es (bus-buses, watch-watches). Nouns ending in consonant + y change to -ies (city-cities), but vowel + y just add -s (day-days). After a number above one, the noun must be plural.
rule_es: Casi todos los sustantivos añaden -s. Los que terminan en -s, -x, -z, -ch, -sh añaden -es; consonante + y pasa a -ies. Después de un número mayor que uno el sustantivo va en plural.
examples:
- I have two brother. -> I have two brothers.
- She bought three watch. -> She bought three watches.
- There are many city in Colombia. -> There are many cities in Colombia.
spanish_note: Spanish marks plural on the article and adjective too, so a bare noun after a number may sound fine to the learner.

id: plural_noun.irregular_plural
error_type: plural_noun
level: A2
title: Irregular plurals
rule: Some common nouns have irregular plurals that must be memorised: man-men, woman-women, child-children, person-people, foot-feet, tooth-teeth, mouse-mice. Do not add -s to the irregular form (childrens, peoples, mans are wrong).
rule_es: Algunos plurales son irregulares: man-men, woman-women, child-children, person-people, foot-feet, tooth-teeth. No se les añade -s extra.
examples:
- Three childs were playing. -> Three children were playing.
- The peoples are friendly. -> The people are friendly.
- Many womans work here. -> Many women work here.
spanish_note: Learners apply the regular -s rule to every noun, which is a sign of learning the pattern.
===
id: plural_noun.uncountable_nouns
error_type: plural_noun
level: B1
title: Uncountable nouns have no plural
rule: Uncountable nouns have no plural form and cannot take "a/an" or numbers: advice, information, news, furniture, luggage, homework, money, traffic, weather, equipment, knowledge. Use "some", "a piece of" or "a lot of" to quantify them.
rule_es: Los incontables no tienen plural ni llevan a/an: advice, information, news, furniture, luggage, homework, money. Se cuantifican con some, a piece of, a lot of.
examples:
- She gave me many advices. -> She gave me a lot of advice.
- I need an information. -> I need some information.
- We bought new furnitures. -> We bought new furniture.
spanish_note: Spanish "consejos", "informaciones" and "muebles" are countable, so the plural feels natural.
===
id: plural_noun.determiner_agreement
error_type: plural_noun
level: A2
title: This/that vs. these/those and one/each with singular
rule: "This" and "that" go with singular or uncountable nouns; "these" and "those" go with plural nouns. "One", "each", "every" and "another" are followed by a singular noun. "Many", "few" and "both" are followed by a plural noun.
rule_es: "This/that" con singular o incontable; "these/those" con plural. "One, each, every, another" llevan sustantivo singular; "many, few, both" llevan plural.
examples:
- This shoes are comfortable. -> These shoes are comfortable.
- Each students has a book. -> Each student has a book.
- I have been there many time. -> I have been there many times.
spanish_note: In Spanish the noun and its determiner agree in number, so mismatches show the learner is mixing the two systems.
