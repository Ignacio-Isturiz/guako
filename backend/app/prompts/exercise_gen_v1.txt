3) "fix": a sentence with exactly one mistake of this rule, and its corrected version.
- "wrong": the sentence with the mistake.
- "right": the same sentence corrected. Change ONLY what the rule requires.

Strict requirements:
- Every "right", "answer" and "right" sentence must be 100% correct English: if you have any doubt, write a different sentence.
- Every mistake must be one that a Spanish speaker really makes for this rule.
- Do not use quotation marks inside the sentences. Do not add explanations.
